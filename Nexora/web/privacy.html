Privacy Policy

Application: Nexora (com.nexora.app) • Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Nexora. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning the collection, usage, and safeguarding of your personal and academic information.

1. Information We Collect

Nexora collects minimal information required to facilitate academic collaboration, note sharing, and verified student directory features:

  • Account & Identification Data: Your name, official student registration number, department/branch, year of study, contact number, and institutional email address.
  • Academic Profile Data: Core slot (Morning/Evening batch), course enrollments, faculty advisor/proctor name, NPTEL registrations, and student club memberships.
  • User-Generated Content: Academic notes, study guides, and optional learning materials uploaded to the peer network.
  • Authentication Information: Secure authentication tokens provided via Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.

2. Hardware & Device Permissions

Zero Camera & Audio Access: Nexora does not request, access, or utilize camera hardware, microphone, or background geolocation. Image uploads for academic notes and profile photos are strictly selected through your device's native system photo picker with your explicit consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is utilized strictly for academic workflow purposes:

  • Verifying student identity within the institutional directory.
  • Facilitating discovery of shared course materials, study notes, and announcements.
  • Broadcasting academic updates and notes notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Ensuring strict read-only auditing for administrative personnel (administrators cannot modify your academic biodata).

4. Third-Party Infrastructure & Security

Nexora is powered by enterprise-grade, encrypted Google Cloud infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure credential management adhering to OAuth 2.0 standards.
  • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted data at rest and in transit (AES-256 / TLS 1.3), secured by strict database access rules.
  • Cloud Messaging (FCM): Push notification delivery for real-time note updates.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Account Deletion & Right to be Forgotten

You have full ownership of your data. You may permanently delete your account and wipe all associated records directly from within the application at any time:

Navigate to Your Profile → Edit Profile → Account & Security → Delete Account & Wipe Data. Confirming deletion immediately and permanently erases your verified directory document, enrolled courses, academic records, and Firebase authentication credentials.
